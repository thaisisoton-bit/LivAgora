The Lavender Shore

★★★★★ 4.8 (312 reviews) · Rio de Janeiro, Brazil · 5-star property
Pool Spa Day Room

Hidden deep within the lush heart of Brazil, The Lavender Shore was created as a sanctuary for those who seek quiet beauty, thoughtful design, and moments that unfold slowly. Nestled along a private stretch of coastline, the pool club invites you to an unparalleled oasis of elegance and relaxation. Each detail — from the materials we chose to the light that fills every room — is crafted to help you return to yourself. Join us for a sun-soaked day of stillness, or gather your circle for a private celebration.
